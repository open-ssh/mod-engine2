System Requirements

Operating System: Windows 10 (64-bit) or Windows 11

Processor: Any modern x64 processor

Memory: 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB recommended)

Storage: 100 MB for ModEngine2 + space for mods

Additional: Compatible FromSoftware game installed (Elden Ring, Dark Souls III, etc.)
